# Services — HRONOAZA AI Engineering & Reliability

**Your systems should do what they say they do.**

## 1. AI Workflow Reliability Assessment

**For:** AI integration agencies, SaaS teams, and organizations deploying critical automations.

**Starting question:** When a workflow says “completed,” which independent observation confirms the business effect?

**Typical scoped deliverables:** an agreed system/evidence boundary, failure/retry scenario review, evidence consistency matrix, contradiction and uncertainty register, severity-ordered findings, and a technical remediation plan.

**Engagement model:** paid discovery or fixed-scope assessment; specific fees and duration are confirmed after discovery. No promise of full security certification or exhaustive testing.

## 2. Enterprise Reliability & Evidence Integration

**For:** teams that need verification in their actual technical environment rather than a report alone.

**Typical activities:** design operation identifiers and evidence boundaries; add system-of-record checks; correlate agent claims with real operation effects; test crash/retry/duplicate scenarios; establish logging, access controls, evidence custody and acceptance criteria.

**Engagement model:** phased implementation, milestones and customer-funded infrastructure. Independent witness/administration must be staffed separately where the desired assurance requires it. Production acceptance is customer- and environment-specific.

## 3. Managed Reliability Engineering

**For:** teams needing ongoing changes, incident reviews, and verification support after the initial assessment or integration.

**Engagement model:** limited, negotiated support hours and responsibilities. No 24/7 response or availability guarantee is implied unless explicitly contracted.
